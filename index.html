Creative Profitability: gross vs. net of returns

Meta reports the purchase the moment it happens. This view re-ranks every ad after returns and reverse-logistics cost.
Net ROAS = Meta ROAS × (1 − return rate) − return rate × 0.4 (reverse logistics + handling + markdown drag). Return rates and the 0.4 drag are illustrative placeholders to be replaced by real joined data.
Why this screen: Sneha scales at >3× and kills at <1.8× on gross ROAS, so the ads that over-promise get the most budget. Flip the toggle: the "5×" hero creative drops while a plain studio-photo ad rises. [04:11] scale rule · [09:12] "Meta thinks every purchase was a success" · [15:44] "I'm optimising for something that may be wrong"
